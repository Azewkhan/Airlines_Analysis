# ✈️ Airline Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing airline operational and revenue data to identify strategies for improving **profitability and occupancy rates**. The analysis simulates a real-world business scenario where airlines face increasing costs and competitive pressures.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The airline company is experiencing reduced profitability due to:

* Rising fuel prices
* Higher taxes and environmental regulations
* Increased labor costs
* Competitive pricing pressures

The main goal is to **increase occupancy rates** and **optimize pricing strategies** to improve revenue per seat.

---

## 🧩 Objectives

* Increase aircraft occupancy rate
* Improve pricing strategies based on demand and aircraft type
* Enhance customer experience to boost retention
* Identify low-performing flights and optimize them

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Aircraft Capacity Analysis

* Identified aircraft with more than 100 seats
* Compared seating capacities across different aircraft types

### 2. Booking & Revenue Trends

* Analyzed ticket bookings over time
* Observed correlation between bookings and revenue
* Identified peak booking periods

### 3. Fare Condition Analysis

* Compared average ticket prices across:

  * Economy
  * Business
  * Comfort
* Found that **business class consistently generates higher revenue**

### 4. Revenue by Aircraft

* Evaluated:

  * Total revenue
  * Total tickets sold
  * Average revenue per ticket
* Found:

  * Some lower-priced aircraft generate higher revenue due to higher demand

### 5. Occupancy Rate Analysis

* Calculated occupancy rate:

  `Occupancy Rate = Booked Seats / Total Seats`

* Identified aircraft with:

  * High utilization
  * Underutilized capacity

### 6. Revenue Impact Simulation

* Simulated a **10% increase in occupancy rate**
* Observed a **significant increase in total revenue**

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Higher occupancy = higher profitability
* Pricing plays a critical role in demand
* Lower ticket prices can increase overall revenue through volume
* Some aircraft underperform due to limited fare options or poor conditions

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Optimize pricing strategies based on aircraft and demand
* Focus on increasing occupancy for low-performing flights
* Maintain a balance between pricing and customer satisfaction
* Improve service quality for better customer retention
